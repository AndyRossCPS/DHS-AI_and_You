2.5 - Train a Human Neural Network

In Lesson 2.4, you chose the weights. Real AI does not work that way.

A real neural network starts with bad weights. It looks at an example and makes a guess. If the guess is wrong, it fixes its weights a little. Then it looks at the next example. After many examples, the weights are good. Nobody chose them. The network learned them.

Today, the class is a neuron that learns. You will start it with the wrong idea on purpose, and watch it fix itself.

Today’s Plan (40 minutes)

  • 🔥 Warm Up Fighter or psychic? 5 min
  • 👥 Learn Together The fix rule 8 min
  • 🛠️ Try It Train the class neuron 20 min
  • 💬 Talk About It Was it wrong? 7 min
  • 🏋️ training: showing an AI many examples so it can fix its weights
  • 🧪 test data: new examples the AI never trained on, used to check what it learned
  • ➕ bias: in a neuron, an extra number that is always added to the total
  • ⚖️ weight: a number that says how much an input matters

Printable lesson sheet - the sheet you write on today.

Pokémon cards: print one set for each team.

🔥 Warm Up

Pokémon are creatures from a video game. Every Pokémon has a type.

  • Fighting Pokémon punch and kick. They have high Attack.
  • Psychic Pokémon use the power of their minds. They have high Special Attack.

Look at these two cards. What type is each one? Tell a partner why.

Attack Special Attack
Hitmonchan ★★★★☆ (4) ★☆☆☆☆ (1)
Gardevoir ★★☆☆☆ (2) ★★★★☆ (4)

Hitmonchan is Fighting: lots of Attack. Gardevoir is Psychic: lots of Special Attack.

You used a rule in your head: compare the two numbers. Can a neuron learn that rule from examples, without being told?

👥 Learn Together

2.5.1 - The Class Neuron

The neuron has two inputs and three numbers it can change:

Inputs (from the card) The neuron’s numbers
A = Attack stars (1 to 5) Attack weight
S = Special Attack stars (1 to 5) Special weight
bias (always added)

\[\text{total} = (A \times \text{Attack weight}) + (S \times \text{Special weight}) + \text{bias}\]

  • If the total is more than 0, the neuron says FIGHTING.
  • If the total is 0 or less, the neuron says PSYCHIC.

(The bias does the same job as the threshold in Lesson 2.4.)

We start the neuron backwards:

Attack weight \(= -1\) \(\qquad\) Special weight \(= +1\) \(\qquad\) Bias \(= 0\)

This neuron thinks that strong punchers are psychic! It is wrong on purpose. Watch it learn.

2.5.2 - The Fix Rule

After each card, check the answer. Then follow the fix rule.

What happened What to do
✅ The neuron was right Change nothing.
❌ It said PSYCHIC, but the card is FIGHTING ADD: Attack weight + A, Special weight + S, bias + 1
❌ It said FIGHTING, but the card is PSYCHIC SUBTRACT: Attack weight − A, Special weight − S, bias − 1

Machop has A = 3 and S = 1.

  1. Total \(= (3 \times -1) + (1 \times 1) + 0 = -3 + 1 + 0 = -2\)
  2. \(-2\) is not more than 0, so the neuron says PSYCHIC.
  3. Flip the card: Machop is FIGHTING. Wrong!
  4. It said PSYCHIC but the card is FIGHTING, so ADD:
    • Attack weight: \(-1 + 3 = 2\)
    • Special weight: \(1 + 1 = 2\)
    • Bias: \(0 + 1 = 1\)

🛠️ Try It

2.5.3 - Train the Class Neuron

Each person has a job.

Job What you do
🃏 Card dealer Read the card’s name and stars out loud. Keep the type hidden.
⚖️ Weight keepers Each one holds one number (Attack weight, Special weight, or bias) on a whiteboard or paper. Change it after a mistake.
🧠 The neuron Calculate the total out loud. Say FIGHTING or PSYCHIC.
✅ Checker Flip the card. Say “right” or “wrong.” If wrong, say ADD or SUBTRACT.
📊 Scorekeeper Count the mistakes in each pass. Write them on the board.
  1. Write the starting weights on the board: \(-1\), \(+1\), \(0\).
  2. Go through the training cards in order: T1, T2, … T10. For each card: neuron, checker, fix rule.
  3. Everyone writes each step on the sheet.
  4. After T10, the scorekeeper writes the number of mistakes. That was pass 1.
  5. Do pass 2 with the same cards. Keep going until a whole pass has 0 mistakes.
  6. Now use the test cards, X1 to X6. Do not fix the weights on a test! Just record what the neuron says.

The neuron got Primeape, Mewtwo, and Hypno right.

Then look at the last three:

  • Gallade is Psychic and Fighting. The neuron said Fighting.
  • Lucario is a Fighting type with very high Special Attack. The neuron said Psychic.
  • Medicham is Fighting and Psychic. The neuron said Psychic.

The neuron only knows two numbers. It never saw a Pokémon with two types. It did its best with what it learned.

💬 Talk About It

The neuron said Gallade is a Fighting type. Gallade is Psychic and Fighting. Was the neuron wrong, or was the question wrong?

  • The neuron was / was not wrong about Gallade, because …
  • The neuron learned the rule … without anyone telling it.
  • To do better, the neuron needs to know …
  • A real AI that makes decisions about people with only two numbers would be a problem because …

  2. The picture shows every training card. The black line is the neuron’s line. The neuron calls the cards on one side FIGHTING. It calls the cards on the other side PSYCHIC. Watch the line move when the neuron makes a mistake.
  3. Press Test on all 140 Fighting and Psychic Pokémon. How many does the neuron get right?
  4. On your sheet, draw the neuron’s line on the graph.

■ Fighting card   ● Psychic card   — the neuron's line

At the end, the weights are 3, −4, and 0. So the total is \(3A - 4S\). The neuron says FIGHTING when \(3A - 4S > 0\), which means \(3A > 4S\).

That is a line on the graph! The neuron learned: if Attack is a lot bigger than Special Attack, it is a fighter. Nobody told it that rule.

Two teams train the neuron at the same time with the same cards. Team 1 starts with the backwards weights. Team 2 starts with all weights at 0. Who needs fewer mistakes? Do they end with the same weights? Do both get the test cards right?

Pick two groups that two numbers can tell apart. For example: basketball players and soccer players (height and weight), or fruits and vegetables (sweet and crunchy, from 1 to 5). Make 10 cards. Train the class neuron on your deck.
